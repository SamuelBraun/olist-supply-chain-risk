6 / What the analysis revealed
07 / 10

Three findings of the Seller Risk Index

Each finding comes from combining signals, not from any single KPI.

01 · Signal independence
Risk is three separate problems, not one.
The three risks barely overlap. The strongest correlation between any two is only 0.21. A seller can do everything right and still be a hidden single point of failure. No single KPI catches all three.
02 · Supply-network graph
The biggest sellers aren't the irreplaceable ones.
Ranking by size just returns the biggest accounts again. We surface small sellers no competitor can replace, and 13 of them have no backup anywhere.
03 · Customer sentiment
Reviews confirm trouble, they don't predict it.
Our model reads reviews almost perfectly, but sentiment tracks sales instead of leading them. Delays move first.
Why we built one number
The three signals are independent, so no single metric ranks risk alone. We fold delivery, satisfaction and structural importance into one weighted score per seller.

9 / Close & next steps
10 / 10

Productionise prototypes on Databricks.

What we built, and what's next.

What we built

  • One reproducible PySpark pipeline
  • Four prototypes: Demand · Sentiment · Supply-Network Graph · Risk Archetypes
  • One unified deliverable: the Seller Risk Index, a weekly watchlist with archetype and action
  • A Structured Streaming prototype re-expressing weekly scoring as a live stream

What's next

  • Calibrate and back-test the Index against realised churn/failure, then tune weights and band cut-offs
  • Productionise on Databricks / EMR as a scheduled weekly Spark job
  • Wire the watchlist into the seller-success CRM
  • Expand the graph: add category, postal region and shippers
From a list of sellers to a managed network, and from post-complaint discovery to weekly intervention.
